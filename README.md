# The Last Portal
The morning after Yushi’s defeat, your parents explain that Sincina opened the portals and brought the monsters into your world. They think it’s over—but that night, a blue portal appears outside your home. Sincina’s voice comes through: “Yushi was only the beginning.”
You enter the portal and chase her through Rooms 10–13. Each room has a different look, a dangerous monster, and a token to collect. Along the way, you meet peaceful survivors who reveal Sincina is building one huge gate to bring the monster world into your village.
The four tokens unlock the gate’s control room in Room 14. There, Sincina fights you while Yushi’s shadow tries to return. When you defeat them, you use the tokens to shut the gate for good.
You make it home as the portals disappear. Your parents are waiting, and the village is safe.
# Play for free
The game is a simple HTML and JavaScript project. There is no build step, software purchase, or package installation needed.

On your computer: download or clone the project and open index.html in a modern browser.
On GitHub: put index.html in the repository, enable GitHub Pages for the repository, and open the page URL GitHub provides.
# How to play
W, A, S, D — move
TouchPad — look around
Click or Space — fire
E — interact with people and objects, or use an open exit
Escape — pause
